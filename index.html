Overview & Scope of the Application

GapScanner Pro is a bilingual (English & Gujarati) NSE stock gap scanner, real-time candlestick & VWAP chart visualizer, intraday & delivery brokerage/profit-and-loss calculator, option chain analyzer, portfolio valuation tracker, and custom price/gap alert application.

We are committed to protecting your privacy and minimizing data collection. Your personal portfolio holdings, custom watchlists, and price alerts are strictly isolated to your authenticated Google account and are never sold, rented, or shared with advertisers.

2

Information We Collect and Store

2.1 Account & Authentication Data (Google Sign-In)

To synchronize your custom stocks, portfolio holdings, and price alerts across devices, GapScanner Pro uses Google Sign-In via Android Jetpack Credential Manager and Firebase Authentication. When you sign in, we receive:

  • Firebase User ID (UID): A unique cryptographic identifier used to isolate your database records under /users/{userId}.
  • Basic Google Profile Information: Your email address, display name, and profile photo URL solely to display your signed-in account status inside the app.

2.2 User-Authored Financial Watchlist, Portfolio & Alert Data

When you actively create or import records in the app, the following data is stored securely in your private Google Cloud Firestore subcollections:

  • Custom Stocks & CSV Imports (/users/{userId}/custom_stocks/{stockId}): Stock ticker symbol, company name, sector, previous close, opening price, latest traded price (LTP), volume, catalyst notes, and timestamps.
  • Portfolio Holdings (/users/{userId}/portfolio/{holdingId}): Stock ticker symbol, company name, share quantity, average purchase price in INR, and personal trade notes.
  • Price & Opening Gap Alerts (/users/{userId}/alerts/{alertId}): Stock ticker symbol, target price or gap percentage threshold, alert condition (PRICE_ABOVE, PRICE_BELOW, GAP_UP_PCT, GAP_DOWN_PCT), active status, and alert note.

2.3 Optional Broker API Feed Credentials

If you optionally connect a live broker feed (such as Angel One SmartAPI, Upstox, Dhan, or Zerodha Kite) or custom market JSON endpoint via the Broker API dialog, your API key or access token is used exclusively within your active device session to request live market quotes directly from the configured endpoint. We do not harvest or transmit your broker credentials to any external marketing or analytics server.

3

Android Device Permissions We Request

GapScanner Pro follows the principle of least privilege and requests only the following permissions declared in AndroidManifest.xml:

Permission Type Purpose in GapScanner Pro
android.permission.INTERNET Install-time Required to fetch live NSE stock quotes, option chain metrics, and sync your authenticated portfolio and alerts with Google Cloud Firestore.
android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Install-time Used to detect network connectivity status and gracefully handle offline/online state transitions.
android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS Runtime (Android 13+) Used exclusively to deliver real-time local notifications when your configured NSE stock price or opening gap percentage alert is triggered. You can revoke this permission anytime in Android Settings.

Permissions We Do NOT Request: GapScanner Pro does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, SMS, call logs, background location, or external media storage.

4

Data Security & Zero-Trust Firestore Rules

  • Encryption in Transit & at Rest: All communications between the app, market data APIs, and Google Cloud Firestore are encrypted via HTTPS/TLS. Data stored in Firestore is encrypted at rest using AES-256.
  • Strict Per-User UID Isolation: Our database enforces Zero-Trust Firestore Security Rules verifying that request.auth != null && request.auth.uid == userId. No other user can read, modify, or delete your custom stocks, portfolio holdings, or price alerts.
  • Schema & Ownership Validation: Every write operation validates ownership fields and server timestamps to prevent unauthorized data tampering.
